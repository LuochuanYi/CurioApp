# Parental Guidance Feature - User Guide

## Quick Access
The **Parental Guidance** feature is accessible from the Home screen's Quick Actions section.

### Button Location
Look for the **👨‍👩‍👧 Parental Guidance** button in the Quick Actions area

---

## How to Use

### Step 1: Select Baby's Age Range
When you open Parental Guidance, you'll see three age range cards:

```
┌─────────────┐  ┌─────────────┐  ┌─────────────┐
│     👶      │  │    🎈       │  │    🚀       │
│   0-3 Mo    │  │   4-7 Mo    │  │   8-12 Mo   │
│  Newborn    │  │  Growing    │  │  Mobility   │
│ Development │  │ Awareness   │  │ & Movement  │
└─────────────┘  └─────────────┘  └─────────────┘
```

**Tap the card** that matches your baby's current age.

---

### Step 2: Explore Development Milestones

Once selected, you'll see expandable sections organized by development area:

#### 📊 **Development Milestones** Section
Shows what to expect your baby to do during this stage:
- Physical Development (movement, coordination)
- Social & Emotional Development (interactions, feelings)
- Cognitive Development (thinking, learning)
- Language Development (communication)

**Tap each milestone** to expand and see the specific skills

---

### Step 3: Read Parent Tips & Strategies

#### 👨‍👩‍👧 **Parent Tips & Strategies** Section
Organized into helpful categories:

**For 0-3 Months:**
- 💞 Bonding & Connection
- 👀 Sensory Stimulation
- 😴 Sleep & Comfort
- ⚕️ Health & Safety

**For 4-7 Months:**
- 🏃 Motor Development

**For 8-12 Months:**

**Tap each category** to see detailed tips and strategies

## Example: Using Parental Guidance for Sleep Issues

### Scenario
Your 3-month-old isn't sleeping well at night.

### Solution
1. **Tap** the 👶 **0-3 Months** card
2. **Expand** the "Sleep & Comfort" section under Parent Tips
3. **Read** tips like:
   - Establish bedtime routine with gentle songs
   - Place baby on back to sleep (safest position)
   - Keep room temperature 68-72°F
   - Use white noise to mask sounds
   - Watch for sleep cues (yawning, rubbing eyes)

4. **Also check** the 😴 **Sleep Issues** card in Common Concerns
5. **Implement** the suggestions that feel right for your family
